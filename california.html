FAQ

Common questions

Are these staff or per diem rates?

You can switch between them above the table. Staff rates are what a staff RN earns per hour on the contract scale. Per diem rates are the contract's higher hourly rate for per diem nurses, about 25% above staff at all three hospitals. Staff is the default.

Which pays more, Kaiser, UCSF, or Alta Bates?

Compare the pay table above step by step, or switch it to years where a contract lists experience. A step isn't the same number of years at every hospital, so check each contract's rules for moving up. Evening and night differentials are shown separately, and some are percentages while Alta Bates uses flat dollars.

How much more do evenings and nights pay?

Each hospital's evening and night differential is listed in the pay table, and the calculator adds it to the rate for the step you pick.

Why only three hospitals?

They're the contracts we've been able to get so far. We add a hospital when we have its real pay scale, and we don't estimate the rest.

Where does this data come from?

Hospital rates come from the contract pay scales, linked above the table. Everything else comes from anonymous salary submissions by California nurses, reviewed before they're published. Rent comes from Zillow Research.

Why are some cities based on one submission?

NursePay is built from what nurses choose to share. A city with one submission is one nurse's reported pay, so treat it as a single data point. Adding yours makes the map more useful for the next nurse.
